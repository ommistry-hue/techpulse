TechPulse Uploader Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

What this app is

TechPulse Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the TechPulse YouTube channel (youtube.com/@techpulse-op). It creates tech news videos and uploads them to that channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

  • The channel owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's permission, to upload videos, set thumbnails, post comments, change a video's privacy, and read view/like/comment counts.
  • No data from any other Google or YouTube user is accessed.

How data is stored

  • Login tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets used by the owner's automation.
  • Video titles, descriptions, preview copies and view counts are kept for up to 7 days in the owner's private database, then deleted.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Contact the owner through the TechPulse YouTube channel.
